Our Commitment to Academic Excellence

At DESMA SPRING WATER SCHOOL, we are committed to providing quality education that helps every student develop academically, socially and personally.

We believe that every child has the ability to learn, grow and achieve great things when given the right guidance, encouragement and learning environment.

Our Academic Approach

Our approach to education combines effective teaching, practical learning, continuous assessment and individual student support.

  • Student-centred learning
  • Qualified and dedicated teachers
  • Continuous assessment and feedback
  • Practical and interactive learning
  • Individual attention and academic guidance

Subjects & Curriculum

Our academic programmes provide students with a strong foundation across relevant subjects and learning areas.

Students are encouraged to develop critical thinking, creativity, problem-solving abilities and a genuine interest in learning.

Examination Preparation

We prepare our students to approach examinations with confidence and a strong understanding of their subjects.

  • Regular class tests
  • Continuous assessment
  • Revision exercises
  • Mock examinations
  • Examination guidance and preparation

Student Support

Every student learns differently. Our goal is to identify students' strengths and areas that require improvement and provide appropriate academic support.

  • Academic guidance
  • Additional learning support
  • Revision and practice activities
  • Teacher-student feedback

Academic Activities

Learning goes beyond the classroom. Students are encouraged to participate in activities that develop confidence, creativity and intellectual curiosity.

  • Quiz competitions
  • Debates
  • Educational projects
  • Student presentations
  • Mathematics activities
  • Science activities
  • Reading and spelling activities
